# Fictional Enterprise VPN Policy

Employees may use the corporate VPN from a hotel or other temporary location when they use an approved company device, a private network, and multi-factor authentication. Public computers must not be used for corporate access.

If VPN access fails, the employee should check that the device has current security updates, confirm the date and time are correct, and contact the IT service desk with the error message. Employees must not disable endpoint protection to make VPN work.
