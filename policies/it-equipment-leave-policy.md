# Fictional IT Equipment Leave Policy

Employees may take an assigned company laptop on approved business travel. The employee is responsible for keeping the device physically secure and must not leave it unattended in a vehicle or checked baggage.

Before travel, employees should confirm that disk encryption, screen lock, endpoint protection, and VPN access are working. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the service desk and the employee's manager as soon as possible.

This document is fictional training content for the IT Support Automation Assistant demo.
