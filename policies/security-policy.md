# Fictional Enterprise Security Policy

Employees must use unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, and approved company systems. Passwords and verification answers must never be shared with another person or entered into an unapproved website.

Access to a business system requires a valid business need and manager or system-owner approval. Employees must report suspected phishing, lost devices, or unauthorized access to the service desk immediately.
